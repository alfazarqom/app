# Decision Policy

Classify every material issue:
- CONFIRMED: supported by baseline, official docs or explicit owner decision.
- RECOMMENDATION: engineering proposal not yet approved.
- OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: business/product tradeoff that cannot safely be inferred.
- UNVERIFIED: external fact/provider behavior not proven.
- SUPERSEDED: prior statement explicitly replaced by owner-approved decision.

Do not invent a vote, approval or consensus. Record evidence and rationale. Preserve alternatives and consequences for unresolved business rules.
